SummaryThe quarter at a glance

Over the quarter, RBNT moved from Redbelly's reserved wallets and vesting contracts into unnamed wallets, and more of it went into staking. Unnamed wallets gained 139.1 million RBNT in total, and RBNT staked in the two Reddex pools rose by 18.2% to 751.3 million. Holdings stayed highly concentrated: the 10 largest unnamed holders held 49.3% of all RBNT in unnamed wallets on 30 September.

  • Staking grew. 751.3 million RBNT was staked on 30 September, against 635.8 million on 1 July. The no-lock pool took most of the increase (+82.1 million). 1,255 wallets had a stake at the end of the quarter, against 1,268 at the start, so the growth came from larger stakes rather than more stakers.
  • The largest holders mostly held or added. Of the 100 largest unnamed wallets on 1 July, 43 grew, 49 stayed about the same and 8 shrank. Together their holdings rose by 45.3 million RBNT (3.3%).
  • Concentration edged up. The top 10 unnamed holders' share went from 48.4% to 49.3%, and the top 100 from 76.6% to 77.4%.
  • One day of reserved-wallet moves. On 10 September, 500 million RBNT moved from Ecosystem (Locked) to Ecosystem 2 (Unlocked), 253.1 million from Ecosystem 2 (Unlocked) to Investors, and 33.3 million from Governance (Locked) to Governance (Unlocked). All of these stayed inside the reserved wallets.
  • RBNT left the reserved wallets through one wallet. Ecosystem (Unlocked), the only reserved wallet that sends its own transactions, sent out 43.8 million RBNT net, including 15 million to fund staking rewards.
  • Vesting contracts released 73.9 million RBNT over the quarter (counting WRBNT as RBNT), and the RBNT locked in the bridge to other chains rose to 43.2 million.
What this report is and is not. It describes what happened on the chain. It does not predict prices and it is not investment advice. Wallets are named only if they are already public: Redbelly's reserved wallets from the DAO treasury page, the WRBNT contract, the two Reddex staking pools, and contracts with a public name on Routescan. Every other wallet appears only inside a group.

Five words you will see

Wallet
An address on the Redbelly chain that can hold RBNT. One person can have many wallets, and one wallet (an exchange, for example) can hold tokens for many people. So wallet counts are not people counts.
WRBNT
"Wrapped RBNT": RBNT locked in a contract and given back as a token that apps can use. 1 WRBNT is always backed by 1 RBNT. We count each token once.
Staking
Locking RBNT in a Reddex pool to earn RBNT rewards. The no-lock pool lets you withdraw at any time. The 365-day pool locks each deposit for a year.
Snapshot
The chain's state at one exact block. We compare the last block before 1 July with the last block before 1 October.
Holdings
For each wallet: plain RBNT + WRBNT + RBNT it has staked. Staking does not reduce what a wallet holds in this report.

What moved, and when

Six of the nine reserved wallets are multisig wallets that moved only on 10 September 2026, in three transfers between reserved wallets within three minutes (blocks 3175499, 3175501 and 3175505):

  • Ecosystem 2 (Unlocked) → Investors: 253,115,670 RBNT
  • Ecosystem (Locked) → Ecosystem 2 (Unlocked): 500,000,000 RBNT
  • Governance (Locked) → Governance (Unlocked): 33,333,333 RBNT

None of these RBNT left the reserved wallets during the quarter. TEAM (Locked), Reserve and USYD/CSIRO (Locked) did not change at all, and the Investors wallet did not send anything on before 30 September.

Ecosystem (Unlocked) is the one reserved wallet that sends its own transactions (118 in the quarter). Its holdings fell by 43,820,074 RBNT. Where the RBNT went:

  • 25,100,000 RBNT wrapped into WRBNT, and 34,720,848 WRBNT then sent to 2 smart contracts
  • 15,000,000 RBNT to the two Reddex pools as reward funds
  • 3,496,669 RBNT to 13 unnamed wallets in 22 transactions
  • 200,000 RBNT to other smart contracts
  • 15,724 RBNT in transaction fees

It also received 9,613,167 WRBNT from a token vesting contract.

Bridge

The RBNT Native OFT Adapter is the contract that locks RBNT on Redbelly when it is bridged to another chain. It held 38.3 million RBNT on 1 July and 43.2 million on 30 September, a net increase of 4.9 million. So slightly more RBNT moved out to other chains than came back. RBNT on other chains is outside the scope of this report.

Exchanges

When the data was collected (26 October 2026), Routescan showed no public exchange labels for any Redbelly address. Exchange wallets therefore cannot be told apart from other wallets in this edition, and any exchange balances are inside the "unnamed wallets" group. If Routescan adds exchange labels, the next edition will report their flows separately.

In plain wordsThis quarter, the big reserved-wallet moves were transfers between Redbelly's own wallets on one day. The RBNT that actually left the reserved wallets came from Ecosystem (Unlocked), which pays staking rewards and makes other ecosystem payments.

Section 6How this report was made

Snapshots. Balances were read directly from the Redbelly chain at block 3025809 (the last block before 1 July 2026, 30 Jun 23:57:19 UTC) and block 3209414 (the last block of the quarter, 30 Sep 23:57:35 UTC). The treasury page lists original allocations, so it was used only for wallet names and addresses.

Which wallets. Every address that appeared in any transaction from the start of the chain up to 26 October 2026 was checked, along with the WRBNT holder list from Routescan. That is 738,504 addresses, of which 160,262 held RBNT at one of the two blocks.

Counting once. A wallet's holdings = its plain RBNT + its WRBNT + the RBNT it has staked in either Reddex pool. The RBNT inside the WRBNT contract is not counted again, because it is the backing of everyone's WRBNT. A pool's RBNT beyond its totalStake() is shown as reward funds. Pool balances therefore include reward funds as well as stakes.

Groups. Only publicly named wallets are named. Contracts are grouped by matching their code to verified contracts on Routescan (TokenVestingManager, RBNTNativeOFTAdapter). Every other wallet is reported only in groups. A holder is an unnamed wallet with more than 10 RBNT.

Checks. The appendix lists the reconciliation checks. WRBNT held = WRBNT supply = RBNT in the WRBNT contract, and the sum of stakers' balances = each pool's totalStake(). Both match to the wei at both blocks. The RBNT read at the start block is 99.63% of what should exist (10,000M allocated minus 30.1 million burned as fees since launch). Over the quarter, the total read fell by 1.45 million while 2.00 million was burned as fees. The 0.55 million difference is RBNT that moved in from addresses this method does not reach, such as contracts only ever called by other contracts. It is too small to change any finding.

The full method, scripts and data are in the repository (METHOD.md, scripts/, data/). Running the same scripts with new snapshot blocks produces the next edition.
