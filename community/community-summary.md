## Short version (for Discord, the forum or a newsletter)

The DAO's first quarterly on-chain holder report covers Q3 2026 (1 July to 30 September). It uses only data read from Redbelly mainnet at two snapshot blocks, with every figure traceable to the chain.

Staking grew over the quarter: 751.3M RBNT was staked in the two Reddex pools at the end, against 635.8M at the start. Among the 100 largest unnamed wallets on 1 July, 43 grew their holdings, 49 stayed about the same and 8 shrank. Holdings stay concentrated: the top 10 unnamed wallets hold 48.1% of RBNT in unnamed wallets.

Redbelly's reserved wallets moved on one day, 10 September, and only between themselves (500M from Ecosystem (Locked) to Ecosystem 2 (Unlocked), 253.1M on to Investors, and 33.3M between the Governance wallets). The RBNT that left the reserved wallets came from Ecosystem (Unlocked), 43.8M net, including 15M added to the staking pools for rewards.

The report describes what happened on the chain and does not predict prices. The method and data are published so the next edition can be produced the same way. Full report: https://redbelly-quarterly-holder-review.vercel.app
